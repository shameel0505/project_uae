# RAW DATA REPOSITORY: 2026-11-09

This folder stores all raw, unprocessed engineering data collected on 9 November 2026 for the Nabat TerraPod Challenge Phase 1 Deck.

## Subdirectories & Contents:
* `caliper_mass/`: Raw CSV files and digital balance exports for pod dimensions and weights.
* `mechanical_tests/`: Force-displacement logs from crush strength and drop-test video frames.
* `moisture_dew/`: Hourly mass logs for water uptake, swelling percentages, and disintegration.
* `germination/`: Daily seed count logs across 4 replicates x 25 seeds.
* `quotes/`: Attached written PDF/image quotations from verified suppliers.
