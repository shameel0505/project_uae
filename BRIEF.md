## 2. OFFICIAL DECK SECTION CHECKLIST (MANDATORY SECTIONS)

As published by ASPIRE/ChallengeON, the 8–10 slides must explicitly cover these 8 core sections:

1. **Team Overview & Relevant Experience:** Who you are, core competencies, execution capability, and team representative.
2. **Seed-Carrier Composition & Mechanics:** Dimensions, weight, formulation, number of seeds per species per carrier, preparation methods, deployment methodology, and compatibility with UAV seeding mechanisms.
3. **Historical Evidence & Track Record:** Past deployment data, test results, benchmarks, and credible evidence (or structured experimental baseline).
4. **Germination Performance Data:** Performance metrics and germination enhancement compared against bare-seed / adjacent reference controls.
5. **Cost-Efficiency:** Itemized, transparent cost breakdown demonstrating the unit cost $\le\text{AED }0.40$.
6. **Sustainability & Materials:** Rigorous evidence that all binder, matrix, and shell components are non-toxic, naturally soil-native, biodegradable, and free of synthetic polymers or microplastics.
7. **Scalability & Production:** Projected manufacturing capacity, tooling, equipment requirements, and realistic scalability ramp over a 5-year horizon.
8. **Solution Readiness:** Honest Technology Readiness Level (TRL), physical prototype status, testing done to date, and a concrete roadmap to Phase 3 deployment.

## 3. STRICT TERMINOLOGY & GROUNDING RULES FOR THE DECK

In accordance with Shameel's Ground Rules:
* **Rule 1 (Number Tagging):** Every number appearing on any slide must be explicitly marked:
  * `[Measured: n=..., method=...]`
  * `[Modelled: eq=..., inputs=...]`
  * `[Target: design goal]`
* **Rule 2 (No Unproven Labels):** Never use "validated", "certified", "proven", "OECD compliant", "contracted", or "partner" without an attached test report, contract, or institutional letter.
  * *Approved replacement phrasing:* "Formulated from food-grade and naturally occurring bio-polymers; organic fraction fully biodegradable; mineral and char fraction natural and soil-native; strictly zero synthetic polymers or microplastics."
* **Rule 3 (Animal Repellency):** Do not claim capsaicin repels birds (avian TRPV1 receptors do not bind capsaicin). Present animal repellency and mycorrhizal inoculants only as hypotheses with measured test results, or exclude them.
* **Rule 4 (No Unsourced Competitor Slurs):** Never name competitors with unverified performance claims. Present our own measured and modelled benchmarks against bare-seed control baselines.
